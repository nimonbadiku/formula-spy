1. PER-INGREDIENT SCORING
=========================

File: scoreIngredient.ts

What it does:
- Reads the base score from database product_roles[productType].score
- Applies profile modifiers based on hair type flags

Factors:
- Base score: 0-100 from database (how good is this ingredient for this product type)
- Profile modifier (Phase 2): binary flags g/b/n → multiplier 1.0/0.5/1.0
- Profile modifier (Phase 3): compatibility scores [-1.0, +1.0] → multiplier [×0.85, ×1.15]
- High porosity boost: humectants and emollients get extra multiplier (×1.15-1.22)
- Emollient floor: oils/waxes on high-porosity conditioning products get minimum score 62

Scoring formula:
  finalScore = baseScore × profileModifier × highPorosityBoost


2. POSITION WEIGHTING
=====================

File: positionWeighting.ts

What it does:
- Ingredients earlier in the INCI list have more influence
- Uses geometric decay: position i gets weight = 0.85^i
- Weights are normalized to sum to 1.0

Factors:
- Position 0: weight 1.0 (highest influence)
- Each subsequent position: weight × 0.85
- Normalization: all weights sum to 1.0

Purpose: Prevents long INCI lists from diluting the score of key ingredients.


3. PROFILE MODIFIERS
====================

File: scoreIngredient.ts

What it does:
- Adjusts ingredient scores based on user's hair profile

Factors:
- Porosity (low/med/high): affects how ingredients interact with hair
- Density (fine/med/coarse): affects weight tolerance
- Condition (healthy/normal/damaged): affects repair needs
- Oiliness (dry/normal/oily): affects cleansing/moisture needs
- Curl pattern (straight/wavy/curly/coily): affects styling needs
- Scalp sensitivity: penalizes irritants
- Protein sensitivity: penalizes proteins
- Silicone sensitivity: penalizes silicones
- Chemically treated: affects damage sensitivity

Modifier values:
- Bad flag: ×0.5 (penalty)
- Neutral flag: ×1.0 (no change)
- Compatibility score: ×0.85 to ×1.15 (subtle gradient)


4. MOLECULAR WEIGHT HEURISTICS
==============================

File: molecularWeightHeuristics.ts

What it does:
- Classifies ingredients by molecular weight
- Applies modifiers based on MW and product type

Factors:
- Low MW (<500 Da): penetrates hair cortex, better for treatments
- Medium MW (500-1000 Da): surface-level conditioning
- High MW (>1000 Da): film-forming, surface coating
- Silicone classification: volatile vs non-volatile vs water-soluble

Modifiers:
- Low-MW protein on damaged hair: ×1.15 bonus
- Heavy silicone on low-porosity: ×0.85 penalty
- Volatile silicone: neutral (evaporates)


5. BUILDUP ANALYSIS
===================

File: builtupAnalysis.ts

What it does:
- Detects ingredients that cause buildup over time
- Applies penalties for buildup-prone ingredients

Factors:
- Silicone type: non-water-soluble causes buildup
- Oil type: heavy oils cause buildup on low porosity
- Film formers: some cause buildup
- Frequency of use: leave-in vs rinse-off affects buildup risk

Modifiers:
- Buildup-prone ingredient: ×0.80 penalty
- Low porosity + buildup ingredient: ×0.70 (amplified)
- Rinse-off product: reduced buildup penalty


6. HUMECTANT ENVIRONMENT
========================

File: humectantEnvironment.ts

What it does:
- Evaluates humectant effectiveness based on profile and product type

Factors:
- Dry scalp: humectants highly beneficial (+12%)
- Oily scalp: humectants may compound oiliness (-12%)
- Damaged hair: humectants critical for moisture (+10%)
- Leave-in products: continuous effect (+8%)
- Shampoo: counteracts stripping (+4%)

Modifiers:
- Dry scalp + humectant: ×1.12
- Oily scalp + humectant: ×0.88
- Damaged hair + humectant: ×1.10
- Leave-in + humectant: ×1.08
- Shampoo + humectant: ×1.04


7. PROTEIN BALANCE
==================

File: proteinBalance.ts

What it does:
- Analyzes protein load and balance in the formulation
- Applies bonuses for beneficial protein use
- Applies penalties for protein overload or sensitivity

Factors:
- Damaged hair: protein beneficial (+20%)
- Healthy hair: excess protein causes stiffness (-8%)
- Protein-sensitive profile: penalty for each protein (-40%)
- Multiple proteins: stacking risk (-12% per additional)
- Low-MW proteins: better penetration, stronger bonus (+15%)
- Protein + humectant: synergy bonus (+5%)

Modifiers:
- Damaged + protein: ×1.20
- Healthy + protein: ×0.92
- Protein-sensitive + protein: ×0.60
- Stacking (2+ proteins): ×0.88 per additional
- Low-MW protein + damaged: ×1.15 bonus
- Protein + humectant synergy: ×1.05


8. CLEANSER HARSHNESS
=====================

File: cleanserHarshness.ts

What it does:
- Classifies surfactants by harshness level
- Applies profile-aware modifiers

Harshness levels:
- STRONG: Anionic sulfates (SLS, SLES) — strip sebum aggressively
- MILD: Amphoteric surfactants (CAPB) — gentle cleansing
- CONDITIONING: Cationic surfactants (quats) — condition while cleansing

Profile interactions:
- Strong + dry scalp: ×0.78 (over-stripping risk)
- Strong + damaged hair: ×0.82 (further damage risk)
- Strong + low porosity: ×1.05 (needed for buildup removal)
- Mild + oily scalp: ×0.92 (may under-cleanse)
- Conditioning + damaged hair: ×1.10 (repair while cleansing)
- Mild + sensitive scalp: ×1.08 (gentle cleansing)

Additional:
- Multiple strong surfactants: stacking penalty per additional
- Chemical treatment + strong surfactant: extra penalty


9. ADVANCED PROFILE MODIFIERS
=============================

File: advancedProfileModifiers.ts

What it does:
- Applies complex profile-specific adjustments

Factors:
- Curly/coily hair: benefits from slip agents, proteins
- Fine hair: penalizes heavy ingredients
- Coarse hair: benefits from emollients
- Color-treated hair: penalizes harsh surfactants
- Heat-damaged hair: benefits from heat protectants


10. FORMULATION BALANCE
=======================

File: formulationBalance.ts

What it does:
- Evaluates overall formulation balance
- Detects over-cleansing, over-conditioning, low-support

Conditions:
- Over-cleansing: too many strong surfactants, insufficient conditioning
- Over-conditioning: too many conditioning agents, insufficient cleansing
- Low-support: very few functional ingredients for product type
- Excessive layering: too many surface-layer ingredients

Modifiers:
- Over-cleansing: ×0.88
- Over-conditioning: ×0.92
- Low-support: ×0.90
- Excessive layering: ×0.95 per excess ingredient


11. CONCENTRATION ESTIMATION
============================

File: concentrationEstimation.ts

What it does:
- Estimates ingredient concentrations from INCI position
- Uses cosmetic "1% line" heuristic

Factors:
- Position in INCI list (earlier = higher concentration)
- Product type (different distribution models)
- Ingredient category (preservatives typically <1%)

Output per ingredient:
- estimatedBand: dominant/primary/supporting/minor/trace/negligible
- estimatedRelativeWeight: 0-1 (fraction of formula)
- confidence: 0-100
- isAbove1PctLine: boolean
- scoringWeightModifier: 0.3-1.0

Product type models:
- Shampoo: 1% line at position 8
- Conditioner: 1% line at position 9
- Treatment: 1% line at position 5 (concentrated actives)
- Leave-in: 1% line at position 11 (more even distribution)


12. CRITICAL SIGNAL DETECTION
=============================

File: criticalSignalDetection.ts

What it does:
- Detects formulation-level compatibility/incompatibility signals
- These are signals humans heavily overweight

Signal types:
- Sulfate-dominant shampoo for curly/damaged hair → incompatible
- Heavy silicone leave-in for low-porosity hair → incompatible
- Bond repair active for damaged hair → compatible (bonus)
- Protein overload for protein-sensitive profile → incompatible

Signal dominance:
- dominant: 15-25 point impact
- strong: 8-15 point impact
- moderate: 4-8 point impact
- soft: 1-4 point impact

Combined modifier: product of all signal modifiers
- Floor: 0.30 (prevents score collapse)
- Ceiling: 1.60 (prevents score inflation)


13. EVIDENCE ENGINE
===================

File: evidenceEngine.ts

What it does:
- Calculates evidence strength for functional dimensions
- Uses scored ingredients × concentration × tag reliability

Evidence dimensions:
- cleansing, conditioning, moisture, repair, frizzControl
- scalpHealth, volume, definition, shine, strength

Calculation:
  evidence = Σ (ingredient.finalScore × concentration.confidence × tagReliability × positionPenalty)

Position penalty:
- Above 1% line: 1.0 (full evidence)
- Below 1% line: 0.3 (reduced evidence)

Overall evidence: average of dimensions with contributors only


14. PRODUCT SUBTYPE DETECTION
=============================

File: productSubtype.ts

What it does:
- Detects functional archetype before completeness evaluation

Subtypes:
- cleansing: dominant surfactant system
- conditioning: dominant conditioning agents
- treatment: dominant protein/bond/ceramide
- sealing: dominant oils/silicones
- styling: dominant film formers/polymers
- hybrid: mixed functions
- unknown: cannot determine

Detection method:
- Calculate category strength (weighted by concentration)
- Find dominant category
- Map to subtype


15. FORMULA COMPLETENESS
========================

File: formulaCompleteness.ts

What it does:
- Evaluates function coverage, not ingredient count

Required functions per subtype:
- cleansing: surfactant
- conditioning: conditioning agent
- treatment: treatment active
- sealing: sealant
- styling: hold agent

Completeness = (functions covered / functions required) × 100

Grades:
- empty: 0%
- minimal: 1-49%
- functional: 50-79%
- complete: 80-99%
- comprehensive: 100%


16. CLAIM VALIDATION
====================

File: claimValidator.ts

What it does:
- Detects claims from product name
- Validates claims against evidence
- Detects contradictions

Claims detected from product name:
- repair, bondRepair, protein, scalpHealth
- moisture, volume, frizzControl, definition
- strength, shine, clarifying

Verdicts:
- supported: evidence supports claim (confidence > 60)
- unsupported: no evidence for or against (20-60)
- contradicted: evidence opposes claim (< 20)

Contradiction examples:
- Volume claim + heavy oils → contradicted
- Moisture claim + drying alcohol → contradicted
- Gentle claim + sulfate → contradicted


17. FUNCTIONAL EFFICACY GATE
============================

File: functionalEfficacy.ts

What it does:
- Checks if product has minimum functional ingredients for category

Requirements per category:
- Shampoo/co-wash: must have cleansing surfactant
- Conditioner/leave-in: must have conditioning/emollient/humectant
- Mask: must have conditioning or treatment active
- Serum: must have functional serum ingredient
- Treatment: must have treatment active
- Styler: must have styling/hold agent

Penalties:
- Missing required ingredient: -55 to -60 points


18. CALIBRATION LAYER
=====================

File: calibrationLayer.ts

What it does:
- Final score calibration
- Applies evidence-based scaling

Evidence scaling curve:
- Evidence < 20: ×0.4 (very low)
- Evidence 20-30: ×0.5-0.6 (low)
- Evidence 30-40: ×0.65-0.8 (moderate)
- Evidence 40-50: ×0.85-1.0 (good)
- Evidence > 50: ×1.0 (high)

Additional calibrations:
- Completeness multiplier: ×0.7-1.0 based on grade
- Uncertainty penalty: -2% per uncertainty flag
- Final clamp: 0-100


19. TAG RELIABILITY
===================

File: tagReliability.ts

What it does:
- Assigns reliability weights to database tags

Tiers:
- Tier 1 (1.0): Scientific foundation (surfactant, protein, silicone)
- Tier 2 (0.8): Generally accurate (damage-repair, conditioning-agent)
- Tier 3 (0.5): Context-dependent (smoothing, volumizing)
- Tier 4 (0.2): Marketing-heavy (botanical, natural)
- Tier 5 (0.0): Pure marketing (luxury, premium, salon-quality)

Unknown tags: surfaced for review, not silently scored


20. PROFILE-PRODUCT GATING
===========================

File: profileProductGating.ts

What it does:
- Gates relevance of scoring stages per product type

Gates:
- isConditioningRelevant: false for shampoos/co-washes
- isCleansingRelevant: false for leave-in products
- isProteinRelevant: false for low-porosity (protein resists penetration)
- isProteinDamageModifierRelevant: false for low-porosity and shampoos
- isHumectantRelevant: always true (humectants always matter)
- isBuildupPenaltyRelevant: true for low-porosity and silicone-sensitive
- shouldWarnHarshCleanser: context-dependent based on surfactant count


21. FINAL SCORE CALCULATION
===========================

File: engine/index.ts

Pipeline:
1. Parse INCI string into tokens
2. Resolve tokens against database
3. Score each ingredient (base + profile modifier)
4. Compute position weights
5. Apply heuristic modifiers (MW, humectant, protein, cleanser, balance)
6. Run formulation-level analyses (buildup, protein, cleanser, balance)
7. Apply per-ingredient modifiers
8. Compute position-weighted formulation score
9. Apply global modifier
10. Apply critical signal modifier
11. Apply functional efficacy gate
12. Detect product subtype
13. Calculate evidence profile
14. Evaluate completeness
15. Validate claims
16. Calibrate score
17. Return final score + confidence

Score formula (simplified):
  formulationScore = activeWeightedScore × globalModifier × criticalSignalModifier
  calibratedScore = formulationScore × evidenceScaling × completenessMultiplier × claimPenalty


SUMMARY OF ALL FACTORS
=======================

The scoring engine accounts for:

PER-INGREDIENT:
- Base score from database
- Profile compatibility (porosity, density, condition, oiliness, curl)
- Molecular weight classification
- Position in INCI list (concentration proxy)
- Ingredient category and tags
- Tag reliability

FORMULATION-LEVEL:
- Product subtype detection
- Formula completeness (function coverage)
- Concentration estimation
- Critical signal detection (compatibility/incompatibility)
- Evidence strength calculation
- Claim validation (support/contradiction)
- Functional efficacy (minimum requirements)
- Formulation balance (over-cleansing, over-conditioning)

PROFILE-SPECIFIC:
- Sensitivity protections (protein, silicone, scalp)
- Hair type modifiers (curly, fine, coarse, damaged)
- Scalp condition (dry, oily, sensitive)
- Product type relevance (shampoo vs conditioner vs treatment)

CALIBRATION:
- Evidence-based scaling
- Completeness multiplier
- Uncertainty penalty
- Final score bounds (0-100)

The engine produces THREE scores:
1. Formula Quality (0-100): How well is this product formulated?
2. Profile Compatibility (0-100): How well does it fit this user?
3. Recommendation Score (0-100): Should we recommend it?
